# Narration — first week onboarding

Six slides. Each section is the exact text sent to the TTS script.

## Slide 1 — Title

Welcome to the platform team. This is your first week, and it has one shape. By Friday you
will have shipped one real change, reviewed one of ours, and you will know who to ask about
anything. Everything else, the tools, the history, the vocabulary, comes along the way.

## Slide 2 — Day one

Day one is setup, reading, and one conversation. Before lunch, get your laptop, your accounts,
and the repository, and get a single service running locally. After lunch, read the
architecture note rather than the whole wiki, and spend thirty minutes with your onboarding
buddy. Nothing today is graded.

## Slide 3 — Days two and three

Days two and three are your first change, end to end. Pick a starter issue. Write the test
first, then the code. Open a small pull request, under two hundred lines and one behaviour.
Then watch it deploy. You press the merge button yourself. Your buddy is there to answer
questions, not to ship it for you.

## Slide 4 — Days four and five

Days four and five, you review someone else's work. Read for intent first: what is the change
trying to do, and does the code say so. Check out the branch and run it before you comment.
Ask rather than tell. And the one hard rule: never approve a change you would not deploy
yourself.

## Slide 5 — How we work

Five norms, in the order they matter. Write decisions down, in the repository, not in chat.
Prefer many small changes to one large one. Own your deploy and watch the rollout. Incidents
are blameless; we fix systems, not people. And ask early. Twenty minutes stuck is the limit
before you ask someone.

## Slide 6 — Friday

Here is what done looks like on Friday. One change merged. One review given. A check-in with
your buddy. And your questions written down somewhere we can all see them. Then one ask: tell
us the one thing this week that was harder than it should have been. That becomes next
week's fix.
